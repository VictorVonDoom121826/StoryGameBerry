About Me:

When I’m not living my cozy cabin fantasy, you’ll probably find me in the kitchen baking beautiful cakes just because I can.

Layers, frosting, little decorations, the whole thing — if it looks too pretty to eat, I probably made it.

And, of course, life is even better with my girlfriend by my side. She’s beautiful, she’s amazing, and somehow makes everything feel a little more perfect.

Now how this game exists is through the power of my mind and a bit of determination.This game was hard to work on since I didn't really have the patience for it. But I sucked in my gut and just started flowing through the code like a feather in the wind.

And while this WAS hard, I had fun making this code. I hope you have/had fun playing this passion of mine.
